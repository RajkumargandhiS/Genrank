THE CHALLENGE

Knowing whether a brand appears is only the first step.

Communications teams also need to understand which sources shape recommendations, where competitors have stronger visibility, which journalists are relevant and how to turn those findings into useful media action.

↗GenRank closes the gap between visibility insight and PR execution.From diagnosis to evidence-led action, with human judgment kept in the loop.

END-TO-END WORKFLOW

Six stages. One connected workflow.

Context carries forward
01 / DETECT

Find the visibility gap

Compare the brand's recommendation share with competitors across selected AI models and high-intent prompts.

Output: visibility gap and priority prompts
02 / TRACE

Understand the sources

Inspect publications and communities that recur in recommendation environments and review the evidence behind each signal.

Output: ranked source evidence
03 / DECIDE

Prioritize what matters

Turn the evidence into ranked PR opportunities. The analyst can approve, refine or dismiss a recommendation.

Output: human-reviewed opportunity
04 / MATCH

Find the right journalist

Connect the story angle to journalists whose beats and recent coverage are relevant to the opportunity.

Output: selected media contact
05 / CREATE

Build an informed pitch

Generate an editable draft from the source, opportunity and journalist context. Nothing is sent automatically.

Output: human-reviewed pitch draft
06 / LEARN

Measure directional change

Compare baseline and later indicators, then capture what the team validated, selected and reviewed.

Output: directional measurement and learning
